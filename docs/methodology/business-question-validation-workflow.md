# Business Question Validation Workflow

## Purpose

Before any analysis begins, each approved business question must be validated against the available data.

The objective is to confirm that the required datasets, columns and relationships exist before writing analytical SQL.

---

## Workflow

Business Question

↓

Identify required datasets

↓

Identify required columns

↓

Validate column availability

↓

Validate table relationships

↓

Determine whether the question can be answered

↓

Approve for analysis

---

## Expected Output

Each business question is classified as one of the following:

- Approved for analysis
- Partially supported
- Not supported

Only approved questions proceed to the analysis phase.
