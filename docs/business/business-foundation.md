# Business Foundation

## Project

**Title**

NeoBank User Engagement Analysis

**Subtitle**

Understanding user engagement to improve retention and reduce churn.

**Company**

Anonymized NeoBank

**Industry**

FinTech / Digital Banking

---

## Business Problem

NeoBank wants to understand which customer behaviours drive engagement and retention in order to reduce churn.

---

## Business Goal

Understand which customer behaviours drive engagement and retention in order to reduce churn.

---

## Current Situation

The bank wants to understand which customer behaviours drive engagement, retention and churn but does not yet understand which customer behaviours are associated with better long-term outcomes.

---

## Project Scope

- Analyse user behavioural data.
- Focus on engagement, retention and churn.
- Produce evidence-based product recommendations.

---

## Objectives

- Identify drivers of engagement.
- Identify drivers of retention.
- Understand churn behaviour.
- Recommend product improvements.

---

## Success Metrics

- Engagement
- Retention
- Churn

---

## Key Business Metrics

### Engagement

Users perform regular financial transactions.

### Retention

Users continue transacting over time.

### Churn

Users become inactive during the observation period.

*The churn definition will be formally established during the analysis phase.*

---

## Why this Project

The objective is to identify behavioural drivers of engagement and retention and translate analytical findings into practical product recommendations.
